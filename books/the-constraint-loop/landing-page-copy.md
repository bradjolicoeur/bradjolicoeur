## Full Back-Cover Blurb

At Apex Analytics, the mandate sounds simple: use AI to triple software delivery speed. For a moment, it works. Code pours in faster than ever. Backlogs shrink. Executives celebrate.

Then the system starts to crack.

Pull requests pile up faster than anyone can review them. Production incidents multiply. Product teams keep feeding the machine with half-formed ideas. The engineers who were supposed to be freed by AI are now buried by it. Marcus Vance, an engineering director caught between boardroom pressure and team burnout, can feel the organization accelerating straight into a wall.

What happens when the bottleneck moves faster than the company can think?

As trust erodes and every “efficiency gain” seems to make the work more chaotic, Apex is forced to confront a harder truth: speed is not the same as flow, and more output is not the same as more value. With the help of an unconventional mentor and the lens of Theory of Constraints, Marcus and his team must learn to see the real system they are trapped inside—before AI turns a delivery problem into a business crisis.

*The Constraint Loop* is a timely business novel about software, leadership, and the dangerous gap between generating more and delivering better.

## Why This Book / Who It’s For

This is a novel for leaders living inside the current wave of AI-driven software change and trying to separate real leverage from local optimization. It speaks to the gap between what looks faster on a dashboard and what actually improves delivery, quality, and trust.

It is especially suited for readers who want business ideas embedded in a credible story rather than a prescriptive manual. The book uses narrative to explore how bottlenecks shift, how incentives distort systems, and why velocity without flow can make an organization more fragile instead of more effective.

Recommended for:
- Engineering leaders and CTOs navigating AI-assisted development
- Product and platform leaders responsible for delivery outcomes across teams
- Readers of Theory of Constraints, especially those interested in modern software systems
- Fans of business novels such as *The Goal* or *The Phoenix Project*
- Executives asking how AI changes throughput, trust, governance, and operating rhythm

## Companion Workbook

For leadership teams that want to turn the book’s ideas into an operating conversation, there is also a companion workbook: *The Constraint Loop Workbook: CTO and Engineering Leadership Companion*.

It is designed as a practical working session tool for CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Heads of Product, platform and architecture leaders, and a facilitator who can keep the discussion grounded in evidence. It helps teams diagnose where delivery is constrained, test whether AI is increasing throughput or just inventory, and run better cross-functional conversations about flow, quality, value, and feedback.

The workbook includes self-assessment prompts, leadership discussion questions, and structured worksheets that can be used in a solo pre-read or in a 2-4 hour team session to surface the real bottleneck and leave with a smaller set of better decisions.

## Author Bio

Bradley Jolicoeur is a software architect and engineering leader with more than two decades of experience delivering large-scale systems across startups and enterprises. He has served in roles including Principal Architect, Founder, and hands-on tech lead, and his work spans cloud transformation, internal developer platforms, and production AI agents. A graduate of the Wharton Executive CTO Program, he writes about the intersection of software delivery, leadership, and emerging technology.
